Indian roads are notoriously unpredictable. From stray animals to sudden potholes, riding here requires intense focus and preparation. Here are our top safety tips.

## 1. ATGATT (All The Gear, All The Time)

Never compromise on gear. A minimum riding setup should include:
*   ECE/DOT Certified Full-Face Helmet
*   Armored riding jacket (CE Level 2)
*   Riding gloves with knuckle protection
*   Ankle-high boots

## 2. Defensive Riding

Assume you are invisible. Do not expect cars to check their blind spots. Always keep a two-second following distance and keep your fingers covering the front brake in city traffic.

## 3. Dealing with Potholes and Speed Breakers

Stand slightly on your pegs when approaching an unavoidable pothole. This allows your legs to absorb the shock, keeping the bike stable.

## 4. Stay Connected

If you are riding in a group, rely on intercoms or group-tracking apps like CRUIZR. Knowing where everyone is prevents riders from making reckless overtakes to "catch up."
