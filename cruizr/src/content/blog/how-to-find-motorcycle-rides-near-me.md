Bought a new bike but have no one to ride with? Finding a local riding group is the best way to discover new routes and improve your skills. Here is how to find riders in your city.

## 1. Use the CRUIZR App

CRUIZR features a "Local Rides" radar and built-in [motorcycle trip planner](/motorcycle-trip-planner) where verified clubs post their upcoming public rides. Whether it's a Sunday morning breakfast run or a week-long Spiti Valley tour, you can RSVP directly.

## 2. Check Facebook and Instagram

Search for keywords like "[City Name] Bikers" or "[Bike Model] Club [City Name]". Most major Indian cities have incredibly active social media groups for riders.

## 3. Visit Local Dealerships

Dealerships for brands like KTM, Royal Enfield, and Triumph frequently organize owner-exclusive rides. Drop by on a Saturday and ask the showroom manager!

## Don't Be Intimidated

Bikers are generally the most welcoming community out there. Show up with a full tank of gas, proper riding gear, and a good attitude, and you'll make friends instantly!
