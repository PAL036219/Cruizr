## What is the Royal Enfield Himalayan 440?

When Royal Enfield transitioned to the liquid-cooled Himalayan 450, many riders still missed the relaxed, low-end tractor torque and low-maintenance charm of the original air-oil-cooled 411cc engine.

Enter the **Himalayan 440** (also sharing DNA with the updated Scram 440 family). RE took the bulletproof air-oil-cooled platform, increased the cylinder stroke to **443cc**, tuned it for smoother highway cruising, and kept the seat height accessible.

### Key Specification Highlights:

| Feature / Spec | Royal Enfield Himalayan 440 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Engine Type** | 443cc Single-Cylinder, Air-Oil Cooled |
| **Cooling System** | Air-Oil Cooled (Low Maintenance) |
| **Gearbox** | 5-Speed Heavy Duty Transmission |
| **Wheel Setup** | 21-inch Front / 17-inch Rear Spoke Rims |
| **Tires** | CEAT Gripp dual-purpose block pattern |
| **Ergonomics** | Upright handlebar with relaxed footpegs |
| **Braking** | Front & Rear Disc with Dual-Channel ABS |
| **Color Variants** | Mustang Brown, Sela Black & Nelong White |

## Design & Standout Features

The **Himalayan 440** retains that functional, rugged adventure silhouette riders across the world love:

1. **Rugged Tank Brackets:** Easily mount jerry cans, pannier bags, or heavy-duty crash guards for long-distance touring.
2. **High Ground Clearance & Spoke Wheels:** Swallows deep potholes, gravel trails, and river crossings without scraping the underbody skid plate.
3. **Low & Accessible Seat Height:** Designed so riders of all height profiles can plant both feet flat on the ground confidently at traffic stops or technical trail sections.
4. **Classic Round Headlamp with Windscreen:** Retains the vintage RE rally aesthetic while deflecting highway windblast during long hauls.

## Official Color Variants Breakdown 🎨

Royal Enfield has introduced distinct colorways for the Himalayan 440, inspired by iconic Himalayan mountain passes and valleys:

### 1. Mustang Brown 🪵

![Royal Enfield Himalayan 440 Mustang Brown](/images/himalayan-440/himalayan-440-mustang-brown.png)

The **Mustang Brown** pairs a matte earthy brown fuel tank with blacked-out engine casings, dark frame tubes, and black spoke wheel rims. It gives off a stealthy, military-spec adventure aesthetic that looks tough whether clean or caked in trail mud.

---

### 2. Sela Black 🖤

![Royal Enfield Himalayan 440 Sela Black](/images/himalayan-440/himalayan-440-sela-black.png)

Named after the high-altitude Sela Pass, **Sela Black** is the ultimate stealth blackout edition. Featuring a deep matte black tank, dark side panels, and blacked-out mechanicals, it’s designed for riders who want a sharp, aggressive, zero-chrome look on the streets and trails.

---

### 3. Nelong White ❄️

![Royal Enfield Himalayan 440 Nelong White](/images/himalayan-440/himalayan-440-nelong-white.png)

Inspired by the snow peaks of Nelong Valley, **Nelong White** features a striking urban-camo graphic scheme across the white fuel tank and side panels. It blends classic alpine rally vibes with a fresh modern look that stands out in any group convoy.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the expected price of the Royal Enfield Himalayan 440 in India?
The Himalayan 440 is positioned as an accessible adventure motorcycle, expected to be priced between **₹2.10 Lakh and ₹2.30 Lakh (ex-showroom)**, making it a very value-for-money option compared to 400cc+ adventure bikes.

### 2. What color options are available for the Himalayan 440?
The Himalayan 440 comes in three primary color schemes: **Mustang Brown**, **Sela Black**, and **Nelong White**.

### 3. Is the Himalayan 440 good for tall or short riders?
Yes! Unlike high-seat ADVs, the Himalayan 440 features a comfortable, narrow mid-section and accessible seat height, making it easy for riders between 5'4" and 6'2" to control.

### 4. Can I use the Himalayan 440 for multi-day Ladakh group rides?
Absolutely. With its low-end torque, strong steel frame, and dual-purpose tires, the Himalayan 440 is built for high-altitude mountain passes and rugged off-road tracks.
